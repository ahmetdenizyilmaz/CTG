CTG composer v3 — phrase and melody development

Use Compose > Generate song, Random song, or the Neon Pixel Funk recipe.
CLI compose and MCP song_compose / composition_random use the same engine.
Keep Develop a recurring hook and Smooth melodic and chord movement enabled.
New variation changes the seed while retaining your selected sounds and settings.
All results are normal editable notes. Existing projects keep their saved notes;
regenerating an older recipe now produces the v3 interpretation. Reports include
composer_version: 3 so callers can record which algorithm produced a score.

What changed
- Short rhythmic cells form a one- or two-bar idea, with an ending and a breath.
- The opening gesture returns; answering endings and later fragments develop it.
- A bounded search plans each bar's pitch route toward its destination. It weighs
  contour, chord membership, repeated-note runs, leap recovery and stepwise
  resolution of passing notes. These are preferences, not rigid species rules.
- Sustained/accented notes use the actual chord, including borrowed/secondary
  harmonies. Four-bar answers target the chord root; the last cadence finds a
  nearby tonic instead of jumping automatically to a high octave.
- Bridges stretch and thin the theme. The climax uses fragments and short runs,
  with a rising register arc instead of shifting every note up an octave.
- Phrase-end rests give the rhythm section space; accents distinguish attacks
  from connecting notes. Bass and drums remember a two-bar pocket by section.

Reference study (31 October 2026)
I inspected MIDI editions of Scott Joplin's Maple Leaf Rag and Bach's Invention 1,
plus phrase-structure examples in the University of Puget Sound theory text.
The implementation uses general compositional principles, not copied melodies,
MIDI fragments, a trained model, or a bank of famous-song hooks.

Sources:
https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=23
https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/BachJS/BWV772/bach-invention-01/
https://musictheory.pugetsound.edu/mt21c/SentenceStructure.html

Reproducible study: scripts/analyze-midi-references.py (optional mido dependency).
Downloaded files stay in ignored out/reference-analysis; no reference MIDI is
distributed. Analysis selects the highest-average-pitch track and highest pitch
at simultaneous attacks. This is a crude upper-staff proxy, not voice separation.
It found 762 upper-staff attacks in Maple Leaf Rag and 238 in the Invention.
Repeated four-interval cells: 206 of 247 distinct cells in the Rag; 41 of 147 in
the Invention. Step/repeat fractions differ: 0.302 vs 0.650. These two pieces do
not establish universal musical rules. Their differences argue against one fixed
stepwise recipe; shared small ideas and controlled variation are more useful.

Validation
Tests cover rhythmic recurrence, phrase breathing, bridge/climax density,
consistent bass/kick pockets, harmony compatibility, non-overlapping lead notes,
repeatable seeds, varied compositions, editable round trips and all scale/style
combinations. Render checks detect invalid or clipped samples. These checks do
not establish that every song will sound good; taste still requires listening.
