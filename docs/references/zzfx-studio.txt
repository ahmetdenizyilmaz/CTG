Studied engine/chords.ts, melody.ts, bass.ts, harmony.ts, drums.ts, euclidean.ts,
vibes.ts, song.ts and effects.ts. Progression vocabulary is adapted directly;
other musical algorithms are implemented in Rust from their concepts, corrected
for native instrument pitch encoding, polyphony and named section development.
No original song, recording or instrument sample was copied.
